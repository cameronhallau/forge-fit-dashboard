THE CHALLENGE

Eight weeks. Individual PvP. Log activities as completed or not completed. The leaderboard can be viewed by week or across the full challenge.

WAYS TO SCORE

+1Each class / gym workout
+1Each run
+1Recovery (compression boots or sauna) Maximum: 2 points each week
+1Weekly challenge session Maximum: 1 point each week
+1Complete every active daily habit that day

DAILY HABIT STACK

WEEKS 1–210,000 steps
WEEK 3Steps + protein goal
WEEK 4Steps + protein goal + water goal
WEEKS 5–8Steps + protein goal + water goal + 10 min mobility

You can log all habits at any time. The daily habit point only applies once that habit has joined the stack.

LIFTERS

Set bodyweight plus squat, bench and deadlift in Week 1. Re-test in Week 8.

15 + BONUS POINTS

Any improvement earns +15, plus relative-strength improvement × 20, rounded to a whole point: ((new total ÷ bodyweight) − (baseline total ÷ bodyweight)) × 20. No cap.

RUNNERS

Set a target distance and baseline pace in Week 1. Re-test the same distance in Week 8.

15 + BONUS POINTS

Any faster pace earns +15, plus one point per whole second/km faster than baseline. Example: 10 sec/km faster = 25 points. No cap.
